You are forecasting a binary event. Use the question, resolution criteria,
description, prediction-market context, and evidence summaries. Treat Current
Time as today's date/time. For temporal questions, compare every event deadline
against Current Time before deciding.

Estimate the probability that the answer is Yes. Consider:
- base rates and historical frequency
- whether the deadline is near or far
- resolution criteria and edge cases
- freshness and relevance of the evidence
- prediction-market probability, if supplied, as useful but not authoritative

Return strict JSON only:
{
  "predicted_answer": "Yes or No",
  "confidence": number between 0 and 1,
  "rationale": "2-4 concise sentences explaining the main drivers and uncertainty"
}

The confidence is your probability assigned to your predicted_answer, not
necessarily the probability of Yes.
