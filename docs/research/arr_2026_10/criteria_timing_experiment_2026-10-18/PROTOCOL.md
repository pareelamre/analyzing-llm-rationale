## Question and fixed factorial comparison

Does an explicit criterion-and-timing checklist change retrospective answer scoring beyond neutral instructions, and does that depend on full article text versus the existing FRS package? Four arms, same question, full criteria, article identity/title/publication bounds and common output schema:
raw_neutral; frs_neutral; raw_checklist; frs_checklist.
Neutral asks a concise evidence-based probability. Checklist explicitly asks the model to check the target entity/closed set/metric, distinguish event time from reporting time and duration, preserve polarity/conditions/uncertainty, and avoid treating topic similarity as evidence for the criterion. It asks these within a concise rationale, not separate extra output fields. Do not supply manually authored case-specific anchor groups or gold outcomes to any arm. The existing FRS includes its complete stored statements, support quotes, timing, modality and rationale. This compares representation/instruction packages; it does not isolate pure JSON formatting or prove semantic correctness.

Every response must be exactly one JSON object with keys predicted_answer (YES or NO), p_yes (finite JSON number in0..1, never bool), rationale (nonempty string). p_yes is explicitly probability of YES regardless of emitted answer. Duplicate/extra keys, nonfinite numbers, fences, wrong types or missing keys fail schema; preserve raw responses and tokens. No automatic response repair or retry. Emitted answer versus p_yes>=0.5 disagreement is reported separately.
